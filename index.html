x402: sheet x402-2026-11-16

Every listing in Coinbase's x402 Bazaar with 3 or more payers in 30 days, asked without paying (twice when an answer differs from the listing) and judged by the published rule sheet-2. 5,336 listings from 1,844 sellers. What they answered:

Restated. Published under rule sheet-1, this sheet counted 431 of 5,336 listings (8.1%) as differing from their listing. Judged again under sheet-2 on 2026-11-17, 147 (2.8%) do, and the figures below are those. Sheet-1 compared each listed and live amount in the asset's own units. A seller who prices in dollars and quotes XRP or another floating token at the day's rate was counted above its listing whenever the index was a few days old, and more than half of the listings this sheet counted as differing were that drift, not a higher price. Sheet-2 judges price on dollar options only (USDC on ten networks, by contract), reports every other option's price apart as floating, and holds a different pay-to address against a seller on any option, since an address does not drift. The original sheet is unchanged and still verifies under sheet-1. The restatement and every row it changed.

The fair pick

For a job and a budget, the endpoints that fit, ranked by the published rule pick-1: those that asked what they list first, then those that promise to settle only on delivery, then the lower price. No seller can pay for a place. In the running this week: 5,188 x402 endpoints. The tables it ranks: x402-2026-11-16. Anyone gets the same order:
